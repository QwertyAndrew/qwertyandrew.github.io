    But why?

    Technically... I already have a portfolio website. I created this back in 2023 during my third semester at my university:

    Despite I think it's pretty cool and all, I found a couple of nasty things about the website:

    Firstly, it is not mobile friendly. Back then, I was waaaaay too focused on designing it on desktop, causing past me to forgot about the mobile users.

    Secondly, it is not direct and not detailed enough. For example, the art page where I showcase my art, I only give a description on the art itself, but never about how I manage to have the final product (like what's the modelling process, did I follow the pipeline, stuff like that).

    I, generally hate it, and reiterating my old code would drive me crazy. So I decided to start making a completely new portfolio website that can be maintained easily!

    Research

    I started with some quick research and put my findings together. The keyword here is ART Portfolio. I checked out a couple of portfolios on the internet, my favourites would be from Evan Nave and David Oneacre.

    Once done with the scouting, I found out that in an Art Portfolio, or a work portfolio in general, should contain these things:

    Who are you? What is your final product? Article from your final product? What's your contact information?

    Even when in an Article, or a Blog, that should have the context of the product, the challenges I have faced, the outcome after facing the challenge, and the result (does your product actually SOLVE your design challenge).
