    Introduction

    Technically... I already have a portfolio website. I created this back in 2023 during my third semester at my university:

    Despite I think it's pretty cool and all, I found a couple of nasty things about the website.

    One, it is not mobile friendly.
    Back then, I was waaaaay too focused on designing it on desktop, causing past me to forgot about the mobile users.

    Two, it is not direct and not detailed enough. For example, the art page where I showcase my art, I only give a description on the art itself, but never about how I manage to have the final product (like what's the modelling process, did I follow the pipeline, stuff like that).

    I, generally hate it, and reiterating my old code would drive me crazy. So I decided to start making a completely new portfolio website that can be maintained easily!

    Tiny research

    I started with some quick research and put my findings together. The keyword here is ART Portfolio. I checked out a couple of portfolios on the internet, my favourites would be from Evan Nave and David Oneacre.

    Once done with the scouting, I found out that in an Art Portfolio, or a work portfolio in general, should contain these things:

    Who are you?
    What is your final product?
    How did you make your final product and why?
    What's your contact information?

    Even when in an Article, or a Blog, that should have the context of the product, the challenges I have faced, the outcome after facing the challenge, and the result... to show if my product actually solve a design challenge!

    But what's my final product?
    Well I can use Blender, I can make Arts using Blender, I do tried to learn how to draw things, and I make Games, and Music, and a bit of photography, and a bit of Web Development, hmm...

    I can showcase my Arts, Photos, Games, Websites, and Music!

    With these information I got, time for prototyping!

    Prototype

    To prototype, I use Figma as the main software to create the overall layout first, aka, wireframes! This is my first:

    I found this kinda off, it felt a bit too generic for me. Maybe because literally all Portfolio Website usually have this kind of layout, and it doesn't really representing me much. So, I tried my best to make another iteration!

    I tried to go for something like Pinterest, where I don't want any nonsense when the user opens my portfolio, I want to showcase my cool stuff first, and then if they're impressed, they can look deeper on who I am.

    Additionally, the Disc rotating on the right side seems to be the most impressive instead of a grid layout like Pinterest, I guess it is also a way to show the cover art of that music I made too!

    And as you can see, it is heavily inspired by the pause menu screen of Unbeatable, good game btw go play super fun.

    Based on the Lo-fi Prototype, I can now make a Hi-fi version of it:

    I want to go for the Mirror's Edge UI vibe, which is why the side bar kinda looks like the pause screen UI of that game.

    Development

    "Woah woah woah, you haven't finish the Hi-fi prototype yet, why are you switching to coding the site?"

    Well... I have to admit, this prototype I create was LAST YEAR (probably around summer 2025), and because of university work, I don't have time to continue. And because I need to get an internship, plus my parents were pretty rushed, I must start coding the website, and then fix them later on in the future.
